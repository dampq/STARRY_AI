# Starry AI ✦

**Your Universe, Your Story.**

Starry AI is an interactive AI storytelling web application that turns your ideas into adventures. Choose a genre, mood, and setting to generate a story, then shape what happens next through suggested choices or your own actions.

## Features

- **AI storytelling:** Generate chapters and guide the story with your decisions.
- **Personal library:** Create an account, save your stories, and continue them later.
- **Story universe:** Explore characters, locations, inventory, quests, and timelines.
- **Alternate branches:** Take a different path from a saved story checkpoint.
- **Reading tools:** Bookmark and export saved chapters, with read-aloud support in compatible browsers.

## Built with

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Express, Gemini API, and Supabase. Hosted on Render.

## Try the project

### [Open Starry AI](https://starry-ai-i4fo.onrender.com)

Create an account or sign in, select **Begin Your Story**, and enter your story idea. No installation, terminal, or API keys are needed to use the website.

**Note:** The first visit may take around a minute or longer while the free hosting service wakes up. AI generation depends on provider availability and usage limits.
